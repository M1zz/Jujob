Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-26

Summary

Daily Gush does not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no adverts and no analytics tools, and the app does not send any data to a server.

What is stored on your device

Your name, favourites, history of gushes seen, streaks and achievements, and widget settings (font, colour, background, refresh interval) are stored only on your device. To share this information with the widget, the app uses an App Group storage area on the same device, and none of it is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Third parties and external transfers

The app does not share your information with third parties, and it does not connect to outside services to send or receive data.

Contacting us

If you get in touch through the email or Instagram links on the Settings screen, we use what you send us and your contact details only to reply to you. Instagram is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it has stored on your device. You can clear or change your name at any time from the name setting screen in the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will say so on this page along with a new effective date. If you have any questions, email leeo@kakao.com.

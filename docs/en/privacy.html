Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-26

Summary

Daily Hype does not collect any personal information. There is no sign-up, no ads, no analytics tools, and the app does not send any data to a server.

Information stored on your device

Your name, favorites, viewing history, streaks and achievements, and widget settings (font, color, background, refresh interval) are stored only on your device. To share information with the widget, the app uses an App Group storage area on the same device, and none of this is sent to the developer or any third party.

Third parties and external transmission

The app does not share your information with third parties, and it does not connect to outside services to send or receive data.

Contacting us

If you reach out through the email or Instagram link in Settings, we use your message and contact details only to reply to you. Instagram is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

Deleting your information

Deleting the app removes all information stored on your device. You can clear or change your name at any time from the name settings screen in the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page with a new effective date. If you have any questions, please email leeo@kakao.com.

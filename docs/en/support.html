Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen to enter edit mode, tap the ➕ in the top left or right corner, find "Daily Hype", pick the size you like, and add the widget.

How do I replace "you" in the lines with my name?

Open the name settings in the app's Settings and enter your name. Every "you" in the lines will then show up as your name.

Can I change the widget's font or refresh interval?

Yes. In Widget Settings you can change the font, text color, background, alignment, line spacing, and refresh interval.

My saved favorites and history disappeared.

All information is stored only on your device, so it does not carry over if you delete the app or switch devices. If you did not delete the app, please email us.
